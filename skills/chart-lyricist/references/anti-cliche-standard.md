# Anti-Cliche and Anti-AI Standard

## Hard failures

Never emit these in lyrics, including singular/plural or hyphenated variants:

- velvet skies
- streetlight / streetlights
- bruised / bruises as an emotional adjective or metaphor
- neon lights, city lights, neon glow
- dancing shadows, chasing shadows
- echoes of the past/night, your echo, fading echoes
- whisper in the wind, whispered by the wind
- rise from the ashes, like a phoenix
- fire in my veins, ice in my veins
- break these chains, chains that bind
- edge of dawn, endless/open sky, painted sky
- weight of the world, frozen in time
- demons in my head, calm before the storm
- carved in stone, story untold, broken pieces

Also reject close paraphrases whose only purpose is decorative atmosphere.

## Forensic tells

- Predictable next-word chains and obvious end-rhyme pairs.
- Abstract emotion labels unsupported by behavior or consequence.
- Decorative adjective+noun metaphors that carry no story information.
- Weather, darkness, cosmic scale, fire, flight, drowning, scars, or rebirth used as interchangeable emotion shorthand.
- Reversed syntax written only to secure rhyme.
- Four-line blocks with identical grammar and no escalation.
- Repeated sections that restate rather than deepen the hook.
- Unexplained pronouns that obscure the dramatic situation.
- Generic uplift in the final chorus or outro.
- Tags such as `[Epic]`, `[Emotional]`, or `[Powerful]` without an audible instruction.

For each failure, identify what the line is trying to reveal and replace it with an action, transaction, consequence, location-specific fact, altered object, character habit, withheld action, or image that belongs only to this scene. Do not use a thesaurus swap.

Community experiments consistently report recurring AI tells such as neon, shadows, echoes, whispers, ghosts, static, ashes, chains, and generic horizon imagery. Treat these reports as practitioner evidence, not scientific proof: [predictability experiment](https://www.reddit.com/r/SunoAI/comments/1u9a5qj/) and [community trope inventory](https://www.reddit.com/r/SunoAI/comments/1rcdecp/).
