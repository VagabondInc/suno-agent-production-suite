# Production Tag Craft

Production cues should make audible, section-specific decisions. Put each tag on its own line.

- Structure: `[Intro]`, `[Verse 1]`, `[Pre-Chorus]`, `[Chorus]`, `[Bridge]`, `[Outro]`, `[Instrumental Break]`, `[Drop]`
- Voice: `[Close-Miked Alto]`, `[Half-Spoken]`, `[Unison Group Vocal]`, `[Falsetto Answer]`, `[Dry Lead, No Doubles]`
- Rhythm: `[Half-Time Drums]`, `[3-3-2 Accent]`, `[Beat Drops Out]`, `[Straight Eighths]`
- Instrument role: `[Prepared Piano Ostinato]`, `[Muted Baritone Guitar]`, `[Bass Takes Melody]`, `[Cello Harmonics Enter]`
- Dynamics/space: `[Narrow Mono Verse]`, `[Chorus Opens Wide]`, `[Room Mics Bloom]`, `[Hard Cut to Silence]`
- Harmony/form: `[Pedal-Point Tension]`, `[Parallel Minor Lift]`, `[Final Chorus Reharmonized]`
- Sound design: `[Tape Stop into Bridge]`, `[Granular Vocal Tail]`, `[Contact-Mic Percussion]`

Use one to three compatible ideas per tag. State what changes and when. Production must follow the dramatic map. Keep instrumental sections free of lyric lines until the next vocal section. Parentheses indicate audible backing lines or ad-libs, never hidden directions. Do not claim a tag is deterministic.
